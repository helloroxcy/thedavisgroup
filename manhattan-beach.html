Manhattan Beach

Manhattan Beach is consistently ranked among the most desirable beach towns in the United States. With approximately 35,000 residents, the community is organized into distinct neighborhoods: The Hill (the elevated east side), Tree Section (the green north end), Sand Section (beach-adjacent properties), and East Manhattan (traditional residential areas). The Strand is the crown jewel of the community—a paved pedestrian path lined with exceptional homes that regularly command prices exceeding $10 million. Downtown Manhattan Beach centers on Manhattan Beach Boulevard, featuring world-class dining, boutique shopping, and weekly farmers markets. The area is world-famous for its beach volleyball culture, hosting AVP (Association of Volleyball Professionals) tournaments and drawing athletes from across the globe. The Manhattan Beach Unified School District earns top state ratings and is a major draw for families. As of 2024, the market is characterized by low inventory, high demand, and record median prices for single-family residences.
