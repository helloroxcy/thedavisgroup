Hollywood
Riviera

A hidden gem straddling Torrance and Redondo Beach, the Hollywood Riviera offers sweeping views of Santa Monica Bay, charming mid-century architecture, and a secluded residential atmosphere. The neighborhood earned its glamorous nickname in the 1920s when Hollywood stars were drawn to its dramatic setting, and the allure has only grown.
