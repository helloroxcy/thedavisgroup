Redondo Beach

Redondo Beach, with approximately 67,000 residents, is the largest of the Southern California beach cities and offers the greatest diversity of housing options. The community ranges from oceanfront homes along The Strand to inland single-family neighborhoods, condos, and townhomes at comparatively accessible price points. King Harbor Marina anchors the vibrant waterfront, featuring boat slips, waterfront dining establishments, and a bustling Saturday farmers market. Riviera Village, the upscale commercial district perched above Palos Verdes Drive, serves as the city's culinary and retail heart with distinctive shops and acclaimed restaurants. The Beach Path, a paved pedestrian trail, connects Redondo to neighboring Hermosa and Manhattan Beach, encouraging coastal exploration and active living. Depending on address, residents are served by either Redondo Beach USD or Torrance USD, both well-regarded school districts,verification by specific property location is essential. The city is experiencing significant development near the former AES power plant site, which presents potential for major waterfront revitalization that could further enhance the community's appeal and amenities.
