Rolling Hills

Rolling Hills stands as one of the most exclusive and meticulously maintained gated communities in the United States. This private city encompasses approximately 2,000 residents across roughly 1,500 acres of rolling terrain, where every road is privately owned and maintained, and a dedicated 24-hour security patrol ensures the safety and tranquility of the community. The city functions as a municipality unto itself, with its own city council, planning authority, and strict development codes that have preserved its character for generations.

The residential aesthetic is distinctly ranch-oriented, with a minimum lot size of one acre enforced throughout the community. This mandate creates sprawling estates with expansive grounds, paddocks for horses, and the sense of true country living while remaining just minutes from the urban amenities of the Los Angeles area. Miles of dedicated equestrian trails wind through the private roads and open spaces, serving horse owners and enthusiasts who form a core part of the community's identity. An architectural committee reviews all new construction and exterior modifications to ensure homes align with the established ranch-estate character and maintain visual harmony across the neighborhood.

Rolling Hills has been deliberate in its decision to permit zero commercial development within city limits. There are no shopping centers, restaurants, or businesses within the community—only residential estates and open space. This preserves the private, serene character of the community while maintaining easy access to nearby commercial districts. Panoramic vistas spanning the Los Angeles Basin, harbors, and Pacific Ocean are common throughout the community, offering residents some of Southern California's most prized views.
