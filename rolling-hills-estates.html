Rolling Hills Estates

Rolling Hills Estates is a distinctive city of approximately 8,000 residents that occupies a special place in the Palos Verdes Peninsula hierarchy. Positioned strategically between the ultra-exclusive Palos Verdes Estates and the larger Rancho Palos Verdes, this community successfully blends the equestrian lifestyle appeal and scenic beauty of its neighbors with a more accessible price point and a distinctly family-oriented character.

The community is anchored by the Shoppes at Palos Verdes, a vibrant mixed-use district offering dining, retail, and entertainment options. The area benefits from proximity to Del Amo Fashion Center, one of the region's premier shopping destinations, providing residents with extensive commercial and leisure amenities without leaving the neighborhood. Many streets feature quiet cul-de-sac designs and tree-lined pathways, creating a safe, walkable environment ideal for families. Horse properties are available throughout the community for residents interested in the equestrian lifestyle, and the area maintains the rural, spacious character for which the Peninsula is known.

Rolling Hills Estates is home to one of the Peninsula's most celebrated school systems, with top-rated elementary, middle, and high schools that consistently rank among the state's best. Strong family orientation, active community programs, and easy freeway access via Western Avenue make this a perennial favorite among professionals and families relocating to the Los Angeles area. Residents enjoy excellent access to regional amenities while maintaining the peaceful, residential character that defines the Peninsula lifestyle.
