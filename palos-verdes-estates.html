Palos Verdes Estates

Palos Verdes Estates stands as one of the most prestigious and exclusive incorporated cities in Southern California. With approximately 14,000 residents nestled across the dramatic bluffs and coastal canyons of the Palos Verdes Peninsula, this city represents the pinnacle of refined residential living, architectural excellence, and environmental stewardship.

The community is renowned for its exceptionally strict architectural review process, administered through the city's Planning Board. Every home renovation, addition, and new construction must align with the city's vision of harmonious, contextual design that respects the landscape and neighboring properties. This commitment to quality has resulted in a neighborhood where homes range from elegant mid-century estates to sprawling contemporary oceanfront properties, yet all maintain a cohesive sense of architectural integrity and sophistication.

Residents enjoy access to world-class amenities including the historic Palos Verdes Golf Club, stunning oceanfront bluffs perfect for hiking and exploration, and the charming Italian-style Malaga Cove Plaza, which anchors the community's commercial and social life. The Palos Verdes Unified School District is consistently ranked among the state's best, making the community particularly attractive to education-focused families. With one of the highest per-capita income levels in Los Angeles County, Palos Verdes Estates attracts successful professionals, entrepreneurs, and families seeking the ultimate in coastal California living.
